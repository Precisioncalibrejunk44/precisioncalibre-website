Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

PrecisionCalibre ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, request an estimate, schedule junk removal, or otherwise interact with our business.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information, such as your name, phone number, email address, and service address.
  • Service information, such as the items you ask us to remove, photos or descriptions you voluntarily provide, access instructions, appointment details, and communications with us.
  • Transaction information, such as the service selected, quoted price, amount paid, payment date, and payment method. We do not ask you to send payment-card numbers through ordinary email or website messages.
  • Technical information, such as IP address, browser/device details, and pages visited, but only to the extent collected by the website platform, hosting provider, or tools enabled on the site.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to questions and estimate requests.
  • Schedule, provide, and follow up on junk removal services.
  • Confirm service details, communicate changes, and process payments.
  • Maintain business records, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
  • Protect our customers, personnel, property, and business.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • Maintain and improve our website and services, where applicable.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary:

  • To service providers that help operate our website, email, scheduling, communications, payment, or business systems, subject to their role and applicable requirements.
  • To complete a service you request, including sharing relevant service details with personnel or contractors involved in the job.
  • To comply with law, lawful process, or a government request; to protect rights, safety, or property; or to investigate suspected fraud or unlawful activity.
  • In connection with a business transfer, if applicable and permitted by law.

We do not authorize service providers to use information they receive from us for their own unrelated purposes.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website platform or third-party tools may use cookies or similar technologies to make the site function, remember preferences, understand site usage, or support security. The specific technologies depend on the website features enabled. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site features.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business and tax records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it was collected. When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or otherwise handle it appropriately.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the information we maintain. No method of transmitting or storing information is guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive information through ordinary email or website forms.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided, request correction of inaccurate contact details, or ask us to delete information where we are not required or permitted to retain it. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information when required for legal, accounting, safety, or legitimate business purposes. Any rights available to you under applicable law remain unaffected.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services and website are intended for adults arranging residential or commercial junk removal. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate steps.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices. Review their privacy notices before providing information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as our practices or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy and update the effective date. Your continued use of the website after an update is subject to the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.
