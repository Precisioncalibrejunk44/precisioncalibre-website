FAQ

Questions we get asked most.

Do you come to me?

Yes. We come to the customer. We bring the truck, trailer, labor, and disposal.

What do you take?

We haul junk, debris, estate items, garage and storage cleanouts, office cleanouts, and appliance removal. We also handle yard waste and construction debris.

What can’t you take?

We do not take hazardous chemicals, fuels, asbestos, medical waste, explosives, ammunition, or firearms.

How much does it cost?

One all-inclusive price per job, based on distance, load size, and disposal cost. We confirm the price before work starts. Photo estimates are preliminary.

How do I pay?

Cash, check, or Zelle. Payment is due when the job is complete.

When do you work?

Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. We reply to requests within the hour during those hours.

Can I cancel?

Please give 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel.

Do I need to be home?

No. If access is clear, you do not need to be home.
